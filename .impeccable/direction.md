# Direction contract (site-wide, Experience mode)

THESIS: The site is a photographer's contact sheet. Veer's work (projects and photos) is laid out as frames on a proof sheet, and his picks are simply larger. It refuses the category default: a hero headline with a pill badge, a row of same-size rounded cards, section numbers, mono labels, an accent colour.

OWN-WORLD: A neutral interface with no accent colour (white paper #fff / film black #0a0a0a, greys #525252 and #a3a3a3, rules #e5e5e5 / #262626). Photographs and project images stay in full colour. Square corners. One face, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, set heavy and tight for display (max 6rem, tracking no tighter than -0.04em), plain for text. No mono except for code. Frames sit edge to edge with thin gutters like a contact strip; selects are shown larger. Buttons are flat black or white rectangles with a 1px border that invert on hover.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor sees Veer's own photo full bleed in colour, his name forming out of dots, his portrait and a two-line bio, and one plain action (Email me). Scrolling, they see his selected projects as large frames, then every project as a frame, then experience, education and skills as set text. They open a project, read it, and move to the next frame.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed colour photo crossfade behind everything. Head-and-shoulders 4:5 portrait (7.5rem phone, 10rem desktop) top left, the dot name below it at display size, the bio full width under it, then Email me and the profile links. Credit line bottom right.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) "keep everything black and white" (later clarified: no orange accent, photos stay in colour; no circling), "i like having both combined" (photos + dot name), "make the website just more memorable overall".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
